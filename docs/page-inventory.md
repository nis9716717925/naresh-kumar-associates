## Compliance posture

- Site represents an **individual advocate / legal practice**, not an LLP, partnership, firm, or company.
- Informational content only; no solicitation CTAs, testimonials, ratings, case results, or outcome promises.
- Practice-area and credential pages use **placeholders** until the advocate verifies each item.
- Contact form included as an optional enquiry channel with privacy consent — not a booking/lead funnel.

## Missing information (required before final content)

Do **not** invent any of the following. Provide when ready:

1. Advocate full name (as to appear publicly)
2. Bar Council enrolment number & state Bar Council
3. Educational qualifications (degrees, institutions, years)
4. Professional experience (years, roles, courts of practice)
5. Verified areas of practice (which of the list above apply)
6. Office address / chamber location
7. Phone number(s)
8. Email address(es)
9. Whether a contact form is permitted for this practice
10. Preferred language(s) of the site (EN / bilingual)
11. Portrait / chamber photography (if any)
12. Any awards — only if real and BCI-permissible to display
13. Domain name / preferred site title
14. Whether resources section should ship empty or with sample educational placeholders clearly labelled as generic information
